What a website build covers

A new site starts with what the business actually needs to say and sell—structure and content before code, custom work only where a template can't do the job.

In scope

  • New site build or redesign, sized to the business—not an enterprise template
  • Domain registration/transfer and DNS configured correctly (A/CNAME, MX, SPF/DKIM/DMARC)
  • Hosting setup matched to actual traffic and budget
  • TLS/HTTPS configured and set to auto-renew
  • Basic analytics and a contact path that actually reaches someone
  • Mobile-responsive, accessible-by-default layout
  • Documented handoff: access inventory, change log, and a plain-English runbook

What you get

  • A site your own team can update without filing a ticket
  • DNS and email that don't silently break when something changes
  • One clear owner during launch instead of three vendors pointing at each other
  • A documented path to ongoing care once you're live
  • Direct access to who built it—not a support queue
